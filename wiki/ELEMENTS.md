# Elemental System

## How damage works
Every spell deals elemental damage. When it hits a mob or boss, the game checks the **ElementType.getDamageMultiplier()** method:
- **Super effective (2×):** Your element is strong against the target's
- **Not very effective (0.5×):** Your element is weak against the target's  
- **Neutral (1×):** No relationship

## Holy & Void — Prestige Elements
Holy and Void bosses are **immune to elemental weaknesses**. They cannot be exploited. Instead, they use multi-phase mechanics that require skill over stat advantages.

### Unlocking Prestige Elements
1. Defeat all 4 elemental bosses (Tempest Sovereign, Frozen Leviathan, Elder Magma Golem, Rootbound Ancient)
2. Find and activate the **Ascension Altar** hidden in the Mystic Grove biome
3. Both Holy and Void unlock simultaneously — choose your path
